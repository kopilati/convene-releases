convene Version 0.7.0

Group chat that nobody in the middle can read.

Convene is a chat for you and the people you choose, with no company in between. This page says what it is and where to get it for your computer.

What is convene?

The questions you were about to ask.

Why another chat app?

Most chat apps sit in the middle of your conversations. Convene doesn't. What you write is sealed on your own device, and only the people in the chat can open it. Not me, not a company, not whoever runs the network.

Do I need an account?

No account, no phone number, no email. You are a card: a link you send, or a code someone scans. That's all anyone needs to start talking with you, and it says nothing else about you.

So where are my messages kept?

On your devices and your friends'. Your computer talks straight to theirs. When someone is away, what's meant for them waits, still sealed, until they're back.

What can I do in it?

Talk one to one or in a group, reply to a message, react, send pictures, take a message back before anyone has it, and use the same identity on more than one device.

Is it finished?

No. It's early and in testing. The Mac is furthest along, Windows and Linux follow from the same code, and the phone is by invitation. Pick yours below; each brief takes you from nothing to your first hello.
