convene Test brief · Windows

Group chat that nobody in the middle can read.

Thanks for agreeing to try convene. On Windows it is one file: you download it and run it, with nothing to install. This page takes you from that download to your first hello in about five minutes.

Why bother?

The questions you were about to ask.

Why another chat app?

Most chat apps sit in the middle of your conversations. Convene doesn't. What you write is sealed on your PC, and only the people in the chat can open it. Not me, not a company, not whoever runs the network.

Do I need an account?

No account, no phone number, no email. You are a card: a link you send, or a code someone scans. That's all anyone needs to start talking with you, and it says nothing else about you.

Does it install anything?

No. It's a single program file. It keeps your card and your chats in one folder of its own, and removing convene is deleting the file and that folder.

And if someone opens my laptop?

Give convene a password when you start and it asks for it every time it opens, and again when you've been away from it.

So what's the catch?

It's early, and Windows is newer than the Mac. Nobody has vouched for the file to Microsoft yet, so Windows will ask whether you're sure. Some things will be rough, and finding them is exactly why you're here.

Getting set up

Five steps. Only the second is fiddly, and you do it once.

  1. Download it

    One file, for 64-bit Windows 10 and 11. Keep it anywhere you like: the Desktop is fine.

    Download convene 0.7.1 for Windows

    Your browser may say the file isn't commonly downloaded. Choose Keep.

  2. Tell Windows it's fine

    Double-click the file. Windows shows a blue box, Windows protected your PC. That's expected: this test build isn't signed with a certificate Microsoft knows yet. Then:

    • More info
    • Run anyway

    If Windows Firewall asks whether convene may use the network, choose Allow. Convene talks straight to your friends' devices, and that is what the question is about.

    You do this once for each version you download.

  3. Say who you are

    Type your name, the one your friends will see. A password is optional; add one if you'd like convene to ask before it opens. Press Get started.

  4. Start your first chat, with me

    Press the pencil at the top of the sidebar, paste my card link, the one I sent you along with this page, and press Start. Then say hello.

  5. Hand out your card

    Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar. That's your card. Copy link and send it to another tester, or let them scan the code. Now they can start a chat with you.

Things to try

In no particular order. Poke at whatever looks interesting.

  • Reply to one messageRight-click it and choose Reply. Your answer carries a quote of what it answers.
  • ReactRight-click a message and pick an emoji. Click it again to take it off.
  • Take one backRight-click your own message while it still says Queued. Once someone has it, it stays.
  • Send a pictureCopy a picture, press the plus beside the message box and choose Paste Picture. Choosing a file from disk doesn't work on Windows yet.
  • Make it a groupOpen Details at the top of a chat and choose Add people. The chat says in plain words who was let in.
  • Give someone a faceRight-click a face in the row under the search and choose Picture. On Windows you type the path of the picture file.
  • Walk awayIf you gave convene a password, leave for a few minutes and come back. It should ask again.
  • Look around SettingsPress Ctrl , for sound and your devices.

Keeping it, updating it, removing it

Where it lives
The program is the file you downloaded. Your card and chats are in %APPDATA%\nordhavn\convene; paste that into File Explorer's address bar to see it.
A new version
Download the new file from this page and use it in place of the old one. Your card and chats stay.
Removing it
Delete the file. Delete the folder above as well and your card and chats on this PC are gone with it.
Checking it
The release carries a .sha256 beside the file. In PowerShell, Get-FileHash Convene-0.7.1-windows-x86_64.exe should print the same sum.
