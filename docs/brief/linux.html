convene Test brief · Linux

Group chat that nobody in the middle can read.

Thanks for agreeing to try convene. On Debian and Ubuntu it is a package from an apt repository of its own; anywhere else it is one binary in a tarball. This page takes you from there to your first hello.

Why bother?

The questions you were about to ask.

Why another chat app?

Most chat apps sit in the middle of your conversations. Convene doesn't. What you write is sealed on your machine, and only the people in the chat can open it. Not me, not a company, not whoever runs the network.

Do I need an account?

No account, no phone number, no email. You are a card: a link you send, or a code someone scans. That's all anyone needs to start talking with you, and it says nothing else about you.

Is there a server?

Not one that reads anything. Your machine is a peer: it talks straight to your friends' devices, through a relay when it has to, and what waits for someone who is away waits sealed.

And if someone opens my laptop?

Give convene a password when you start and it asks for it every time it opens, and again when you've been away from it.

So what's the catch?

It's early, and Linux is the least travelled of the three desktops: it is built and tested by machine, and you may be the first person to click around in it on your distribution. Finding what's rough is exactly why you're here.

Getting set up

Four steps. The first is the only one with a terminal in it.

  1. Install it

    On Debian, Ubuntu and their relatives, x86_64. Three lines, one at a time: the first fetches the key the repository is signed with, the second names the repository, the third installs convene.

    curl -fsSL https://kopilati.github.io/convene-releases/convene.gpg | sudo tee /usr/share/keyrings/convene.gpg >/dev/null
    echo "deb [signed-by=/usr/share/keyrings/convene.gpg] https://kopilati.github.io/convene-releases stable main" | sudo tee /etc/apt/sources.list.d/convene.list
    sudo apt update && sudo apt install convene

    Convene is then in your applications menu, and convene is on your path. sudo apt upgrade brings new versions.

    On another distribution, or without the repository: the release carries convene_0.7.1_amd64.deb for sudo dpkg -i, and convene-0.7.1-linux-x86_64.tar.gz, which holds the one binary: unpack it anywhere and run ./convene.

  2. Say who you are

    Open Convene. Type your name, the one your friends will see. A password is optional; add one if you'd like convene to ask before it opens. Press Get started.

  3. Start your first chat, with me

    Press the pencil at the top of the sidebar, paste my card link, the one I sent you along with this page, and press Start. Then say hello.

  4. Hand out your card

    Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar. That's your card. Copy link and send it to another tester, or let them scan the code. Now they can start a chat with you.

Things to try

In no particular order. Poke at whatever looks interesting.

  • Reply to one messageRight-click it and choose Reply. Your answer carries a quote of what it answers.
  • ReactRight-click a message and pick an emoji. Click it again to take it off.
  • Take one backRight-click your own message while it still says Queued. Once someone has it, it stays.
  • Send a pictureCopy a picture, press the plus beside the message box and choose Paste Picture. Choosing a file from disk doesn't work on Linux yet.
  • Make it a groupOpen Details at the top of a chat and choose Add people. The chat says in plain words who was let in.
  • Give someone a faceRight-click a face in the row under the search and choose Picture. On Linux you type the path of the picture file.
  • Walk awayIf you gave convene a password, leave for a few minutes and come back. It should ask again.
  • Look around SettingsPress Ctrl , for sound and your devices.

Where it keeps things, and removing it

Your seat
Your card and chats are in ~/.local/share/convene. An upgrade does not touch them.
Removing it
sudo apt remove convene. Delete the folder above as well and your card and chats on this machine are gone with it.
