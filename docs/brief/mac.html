convene Test brief · Mac

Group chat that nobody in the middle can read.

Thanks for agreeing to try convene. This page tells you why it might be worth a spot in your Dock, and how to get from nothing to your first hello in about ten minutes.

Why bother?

The questions you were about to ask.

Why another chat app?

Most chat apps sit in the middle of your conversations. Convene doesn't. What you write is sealed on your Mac, and only the people in the chat can open it. Not me, not a company, not whoever runs the network.

Do I need an account?

No account, no phone number, no email. You are a card: a link you send, or a code someone scans. That's all anyone needs to start talking with you, and it says nothing else about you.

What about the pictures I give people?

They stay on your Mac. Give Sofie a silly picture and you're the only one who ever sees it.

And if someone opens my laptop?

Convene locks itself when you've been away and asks for Touch ID or your password before it shows a word.

So what's the catch?

It's early. Some things will be rough, and finding them is exactly why you're here. The Mac is where it is furthest along; friends on Windows and Linux can join you from their own briefs.

Getting set up

Five steps. Only the first two are fiddly, and you do them once.

  1. Install it

    Convene installs through Homebrew, a free tool that puts apps on your Mac. If you've never heard of it, set it up at brew.sh first. Then open the Terminal app and paste these three lines, one at a time, pressing Return after each. The first tells Homebrew where convene lives, the second says you trust that place, the third installs it.

    brew tap kopilati/releases https://github.com/kopilati/convene-releases
    brew trust --tap kopilati/releases
    brew install --cask convene

    No Homebrew? Download the zip for Apple silicon or Intel, unzip it and drag Convene into Applications. This is version 0.7.1, for macOS 12 and later.

  2. Tell your Mac it's fine

    The first time you open Convene, macOS says it could not verify the app. That's expected: this test build isn't registered with Apple yet. Click Done, then:

    • System Settings
    • Privacy & Security
    • scroll to Security
    • Open Anyway
    • Open

    You do this once. After that it opens like any other app.

  3. Say who you are

    Type your name, the one your friends will see. A password is optional; add one if you'd like Convene to ask before it opens. Press Get started.

  4. Start your first chat, with me

    Press the pencil at the top of the sidebar, paste my card link, the one I sent you along with this page, and press Start. Then say hello.

  5. Hand out your card

    Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar. That's your card. Copy link and send it to another tester, or let them scan the code. Now they can start a chat with you.

Things to try

In no particular order. Poke at whatever looks interesting.

  • Reply to one messageRight-click it and choose Reply. Your answer carries a quote of what it answers.
  • ReactRight-click a message and pick an emoji. Click it again to take it off.
  • Take one backRight-click your own message while it still says Queued. Once someone has it, it stays.
  • Send a picture or a videoThe plus beside the message box. Pasting a picture works too.
  • Make it a groupOpen Details at the top of a chat and choose Add people. The chat says in plain words who was let in.
  • Give someone a faceRight-click a face in the row under the search and choose Picture.
  • Walk awayLeave for a few minutes and come back. Convene should be locked.
  • Look around SettingsPress ⌘ , for sound, the lock and your devices.
